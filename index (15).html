Inspections and audits

Checklists built for your sector

Each client is assigned a category, and our inspectors use the matching checklist.

Food industry

Manufacturers, bakeries, restaurants, catering, cold chain and food storage.

  • Pest-proofing of doors, drains and vents
  • Storage, waste and chemical separation
  • Hygiene facilities and staff practice
  • Bait station and trap records

Non-food industry

Textile, packaging, pharmaceutical support, warehouses and general manufacturing.

  • Housekeeping and waste handling
  • Warehouse stock and storage condition
  • Perimeter and entry point proofing
  • Canteen and pantry areas

Commercial

Offices, hotels, hospitals, schools, malls and retail premises.

  • Kitchen, pantry and waste areas
  • Signs of rodents and insects
  • Entry points and gaps
  • Staff reporting process

Household

Homes, apartments and residential communities.

  • Kitchens and bathrooms
  • Cracks and entry points
  • Signs of cockroaches, ants or rodents
  • Gardens, drains and cupboards
